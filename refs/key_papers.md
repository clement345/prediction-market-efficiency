# Key References to Read / Annotate

## Fundamentals — read first

- [ ] **Wolfers & Zitzewitz (2004)** — Prediction Markets, JEP
  → Foundational overview. Section 2 on the theoretical conditions for efficiency.

- [ ] **Grossman & Stiglitz (1980)** — On the Impossibility of Informationally Efficient Markets, AER
  → Core argument: if markets were perfectly efficient, no one would have an incentive to gather information.

- [ ] **de Finetti (1937)** — La Prévision: Ses Lois Logiques
  → Dutch book theorem — theoretical foundation of the BCI.

- [ ] **Snowberg & Wolfers (2010)** — Explaining the Favorite-Longshot Bias, JPE
  → Our H1. Check whether the mechanisms they identify apply to Polymarket.

## Methods — semantic clustering

- [ ] **Reimers & Gurevych (2019)** — Sentence-BERT, EMNLP
  → sBERT to encode market questions.

- [ ] **Blondel et al. (2008)** — Fast Unfolding of Communities in Large Networks
  → Louvain algorithm for clustering.

## Benchmark comparison

- [ ] **Tetlock & Gardner (2016)** — Superforecasting
  → Reference Brier scores for superforecasters (~0.15-0.18).

- [ ] **Karger et al. (2023)** — Forecasting Tournaments, Academic Collaborations
  → Comparison of Metaculus / GJP / markets.

## Limits of Arbitrage

- [ ] **Shleifer & Vishny (1997)** — The Limits of Arbitrage, JF
  → Theoretical framework to explain the persistence of BCI > 0.

- [ ] **Gromb & Vayanos (2010)** — Limits of Arbitrage: The State of the Theory, NBER
  → Literature review.
